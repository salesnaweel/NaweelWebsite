Our Story

From Oilfields
to Artisan Hands

We are a sister company of Inpetro Gemilang, a trusted supplier of oilfield equipment since 2006. Built on over a decade of experience in international trade, logistics, and quality assurance — we know what global markets demand.

In 2025, we expanded our vision: to support Indonesia's unsung artisans and bring their creations to the world. From intricately carved wood sculptures to hand-embroidered fabrics, we export the true beauty of Indonesia — with the same rigour and dedication that defined our industrial roots.

Two worlds. One commitment to quality.
